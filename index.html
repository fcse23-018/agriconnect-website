Questions

Good to know

Is AgriConnect free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases in this build. It is a pilot release.

Why does Android warn me about the file?

Because the APK is installed directly rather than through the Play Store, and this build is debug-signed for testing. Android flags every sideloaded app once. The install steps above walk you through it.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. Harvests, expenses, livestock counts, listings and orders are saved on the phone first and queued for sync. They upload automatically when you have a connection. Satellite imagery and the marketplace do need a network.

Which phones are supported?

Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer. The app is built for phones, not tablets, and there is no iPhone version yet.

What happens to my data?

Your records belong to you. Read the plain-language summary in our privacy notice — or the full policy under Profile → Privacy & data in the app.

Can I run my own server?

The project is MIT-licensed. The API, database migrations and Android source are all in the repository, and a debug build can point at any server from Settings → Server.
