Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated 16 November 2026 · Applies to the AgriConnect Android app and API

This is the plain-language summary of how AgriConnect handles your data. The full policy — including the legal wording under the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2024 — is in the app under Profile → Privacy & data.

What we collect, and why

  • Name and phone number — to create your account, sign you in and send verification codes.
  • Email address — optional, for sign-in and recovery.
  • Password — stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash, never in plain text.
  • Farm data — plot boundaries, crops, harvests, livestock, sales, expenses, listings and orders, so the app can keep your records and summaries.
  • Preferences and avatar — optional, to personalise the app.
  • Connection state — so queued records sync when you are back online.

What we never collect

No biometric data, no facial images, no camera or motion-sensor data, no advertising identifiers and no access to your contacts. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Offline-first

Records are written to your phone first and uploaded when a connection is available. If you sign out or delete your account, anything still waiting to sync is uploaded first so no work is lost.

Who we share data with

  • Google Earth Engine — farm plot geometry only (no name or phone number), to fetch Sentinel-2 imagery for crop health.
  • Messaging providers — your phone number or email, only to deliver verification and recovery codes.
  • Our hosting provider — to run the service, with traffic encrypted in transit.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Account details, plots, harvests, livestock and satellite history — until you delete your account, then removed or anonymised.
  • Sales, expenses, listings and orders — de-identified and kept for 7 years for tax and financial record-keeping.
  • Security audit logs — 24 months. Notifications — 12 months. Backups — 30 days rolling.

Your rights

Under the Botswana Data Protection Act you can:

  • Access your data — in the app, Profile → Privacy & data → Export my data.
  • Correct it — edit any record directly in the app.
  • Delete it — Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account. This signs you out everywhere and removes your farm records and satellite history.
  • Object, restrict, or complain — contact us, or the Information and Data Protection Commission.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sign-in tokens are stored as irreversible hashes on the server and in encrypted storage on your device. All traffic uses HTTPS, and access is role-based: farm employees cannot see financial records, and buyers cannot see farm operations.

Children

AgriConnect is built for adults managing farms and buying produce. It is not directed at children.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@agriconnect.bw. We will tell you in the app before any material change to this notice takes effect.
